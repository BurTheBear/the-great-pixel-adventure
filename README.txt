## Project Title
	The Great Pixel Adventure

## Description
	The Great Pixel Adventure is a 2D game, where the player has
	to traverse several different levels to complete the 1910s 
	decade of the game. The game time travels back to the 1910s era
	of World History, and the player has to talk to historical NPCs
	and complete quests to complete the three levels. The levels are
	Mexican Revolution, RMS Titanic, and Henry Ford. Once the player
	talks to the NPCs on the Mexican Revolution level and the RMS Titanic
	level, they have to find 3 tools on the Henry Ford level and then
	tell the Henry Ford NPC. After that, they should find the final door
	to win the game.

## Installation
	Go to this link "https://atharvthegamer1.wixsite.com/the-great-pixel-adve"

## Usage
E: Toggle object
	Enter: Go to the next speech from the NPC
	Arrow Keys: Up, Down, Left, Right
	Shift: Sprint
	Space: Jump
